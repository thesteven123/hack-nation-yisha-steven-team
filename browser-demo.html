The desktop AI research workspace
Our prototype extends the open-source AgentsDock platform with a human-guided research workflow. A Literature Agent searches and reads sources. An Idea Agent compares research directions. A separate review context can ask for more evidence, while the researcher chooses, revises, combines, or defers a direction.


1. Research goalState the question and constraints.
2. Literature & ideasInspect quotations and compare directions.
3. Human choiceRecord a decision and select a next action.
4. Analysis & reviewPreserve inputs, observations, and limitations.


Planner, Analyst, and Reviewer model jobs are separately recorded in the desktop prototype. The currently implemented analysis families are source evidence checks and paired numerical analysis.


This browser demo runs two local calculations. It does not run the desktop Literature Agent, Idea Agent, or native Codex jobs. The desktop workflow requires its independent local service and provider setup.


Team
Yisha Tang & Steven Milligan-Villegas
University of California, Berkeley
We developed the idea together and separately experimented with different agent platforms to explore how AI could support research.


Current limits
Bounded literature coverage; text-only document extraction; limited analysis adapters. Local workflow demonstrations do not establish scientific effectiveness.
